What to Do With Your Pet's Ashes: 12 Meaningful Ideas

6 min read · August 2026

When you bring home your pet's ashes, it can be surprisingly hard to know what to do next. There's no rush, and there's no wrong answer — only what feels right for you. Some people keep them close for years before deciding. Here are twelve gentle, meaningful ideas to help you find a way that honors the bond you shared.

Ways to keep them close

1. A keepsake urn

A beautiful urn on a shelf or mantel is the most classic choice for a reason — it gives your pet a dignified, permanent place in your home. Many can be engraved with their name, dates, or a paw print.

2. Memorial jewelry

A small amount of ashes can be sealed into a pendant, ring, or bracelet, so you can carry a piece of them wherever you go. There are also artisans who blend ashes into glass or resin to create keepsakes with real color and light.

3. A photo-and-ashes display

Combine a small urn or vial with your favorite photo of them in a dedicated memorial shelf or shadow box. Seeing their face alongside gives the ashes context — this was a life, not just a loss.

4. Keep a small portion, release the rest

You don't have to choose just one path. Many people keep a little in a keepsake and scatter or bury the rest — a way to both hold on and let go.

Ways to return them to a meaningful place

5. Scatter them somewhere they loved

A favorite park, trail, beach, or backyard can become a place you return to. Choose somewhere the memory feels alive. (Check local rules first — some public lands and parks have guidelines about scattering.)

6. Bury them at home

If you own your property and local regulations allow it, a home burial in a meaningful spot — perhaps marked with a stone or plant — gives you a place to visit and tend.

7. Plant a tree or garden with them

Special biodegradable urns let you plant a tree or flowers with your pet's ashes, turning grief into something living and growing. Even without a special urn, a memorial garden in their honor can become a quiet place of comfort.

"Some people need a place to visit. Some need to carry them along. Grief has no single shape — and neither should remembrance."

Ways to turn them into something lasting

8. Memorial glass art or paperweight

Artists can fuse a pinch of ashes into blown glass ornaments, hearts, or paperweights — beautiful objects that catch the light and hold a piece of them.

9. A custom keepsake or sculpture

From engraved wooden boxes to small sculptures, many makers create bespoke memorials designed to hold ashes with real artistry.

10. Grow a reef or give back to nature

Some services incorporate ashes into ocean memorial reefs or tree-planting programs — a way to let your pet's memory become part of something that gives life.

Ways to remember, with or without ashes

11. Create a memorial ritual

Choose a date each year — their birthday, or the day you met — to light a candle, visit their spot, or simply look through photos. Ritual gives love a place to return to.

12. Build a place for their memories

Beyond the physical ashes, gather everything you remember — the photos, the stories, the little habits — into one place you can revisit. It's often the keepsake that comforts most, because it holds who they were, not just what remains.
