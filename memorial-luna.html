Her Story

Luna arrived on a cold February morning, a ball of cloud that fit in one hand and decided, immediately, that the warmest spot in the house belonged to her.

For thirteen years she was the quiet heart of our home — waiting on the windowsill at 6pm, curling at the foot of the bed, appearing the moment anyone was sad. She never asked for much. A sunbeam. A lap. To be near.

She left the way she lived: gently, and on her own terms. The house is quieter now, but the windowsill still catches the evening light, and we still look for her there.
